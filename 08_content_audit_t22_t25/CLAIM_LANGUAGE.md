# 论文与PPT措辞边界

## 推荐写法

**English**

> We conducted a structured single-reviewer content audit of all 87 canonical patent families assigned to Topics 22 and 25. Family records were randomized and coded with the BERTopic identity and downstream model outputs concealed. A delayed recoding subset was used to assess intra-rater consistency.

**中文**

> 我们对Topic 22和Topic 25所包含的全部87个规范化专利族进行了结构化单人内容审计。编码时隐藏BERTopic身份及下游模型结果，并通过延迟复审子集评估审查者内一致性。

## 禁止写法

- independently validated by human experts
- double-blind review
- inter-rater reliability
- Topic 22 proves collision risk / cascade propagation
- no propagation evidence proves no cascade exists

## 结果出现前的PPT写法

> Ongoing structured content audit: 87 canonical families, topic-blinded coding, delayed intra-rater retest.

结果完成后再将 `Ongoing` 改为 `Completed`，并填入实际比例与置信区间。
